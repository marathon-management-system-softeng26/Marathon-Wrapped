# Marathon Wrapped

Marathon Wrapped gives Soekarno Run 2027 participants a private race showcase and a 1080 × 1350 PNG they can save. The trusted registration supplies runner identity, event, category, BIB, and any matched photos from the simulated gallery.

The November 11, 2026 decision supersedes chip-first Wrapped generation. October 20 approved a disconnected-only Strava state, and November 2 approved opt-in enrichment of the chip recap. Chip timing and results now serve legacy organizer operations. The showcase reaches `ready` only after the runner selects a qualifying Strava activity (or the system selects the sole fully qualifying match) and the worker stores its projection. The selected activity supplies the performance data. Event placement, percentile, and overtake claims from the superseded chip-first recap are outside the runner showcase.

Runners confirm their Supabase email and redeem a one-time code from their event organizer to claim a registration. Each claimed runner card has a Strava action. An account can reuse its athlete connection for multiple registrations, each with a separate attachment and selected activity. Detaching clears one registration's Strava data. Disconnecting clears the account's live showcases and starts provider revocation. Server-owned access mappings protect runner and organizer functions. Personal showcase links remain private.

The [SRS](docs/SRS_v4.md), [requirements register](docs/requirements-register.md), and [approved November 11 design](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-strava-first-runner-claims-design.md) define the contract. The [acceptance matrix](docs/qa/acceptance-matrix.md) tracks verification. Label synthetic fixtures and stock photos as demonstrations. The local Figma Make export in the primary checkout's `ui reference` directory is a read-only visual reference and stays out of Git.
