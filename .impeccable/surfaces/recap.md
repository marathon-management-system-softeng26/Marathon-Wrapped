# Connected race recap

Mode: Experience for the story; Operate for login, selection and organizer forms.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The runner relives the kilometers through a short sequence whose numbers come from the chip result. Unsupported hydration claims give way to honest optional-data states.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the supplied Figma Make's red field, cream statistic panels, lime emphasis and Bricolage Grotesque lettering. The read-only export is reference only; its portrait and source are not copied. The existing attributed stock race photo is explicitly simulated.

STORY: Sign in, choose an authorized runner, understand finish or missing timing, inspect splits/photos, save a keepsake. Direct page buttons keep photos and export reachable without traversing the story.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Progress segments across the top, race identity, a large left-aligned lime/cream introduction and runner identity. Navigation arrows sit below the content. Mobile keeps the sequence and readable panels without horizontal page overflow.

FORM: User-pinned Figma story composition, code-led extension. seed: none (user-pinned reference). No alternate world or concept tournament.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
